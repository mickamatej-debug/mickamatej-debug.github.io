Privacy policy: Pohádky pro děti uploader

Pohádky pro děti uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Pohádky pro děti to upload and manage videos on that channel. It is not offered to the public.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, to upload videos, manage their details and read channel statistics. It does not collect, store or share personal data from viewers or anyone else.

The access permission it receives from Google is stored privately on the owner's own computer or server. It is not sold or shared. The owner can withdraw access at any time at Google account permissions.
